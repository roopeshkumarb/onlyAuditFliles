- Guard limits NOT tested by any mutation, and cannot be: someone with write access who rewrites the ledger, the evidence, the hash, the chain, guard_report.json AND git history together (a force-push) passes a local guard; "separate commits" holds only while history is honest, and one person can approve all three flags in three quick, visible commits.
