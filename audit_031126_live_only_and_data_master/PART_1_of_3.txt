7. REC classification: config/recoverability.json patterns "tick","tbt","depth","quote","news","chain_snap" etc. = NON_RECOVERABLE. Names like "feed_v3", "websocket", "option_chain", "market_status" match no pattern -> UNKNOWN (treated NON_REC) (OVH rows 26,49,22).

9. i7 recording: OVH says "i7 NOT measured" for fyers TBT, kite ticks, dhan depth. All recorder claims above are i5 only.
10. Recorder day gaps (OVH): 20261020 near-empty for ticks (2,924), TBT (53 frames), Upstox (297 frames); Dhan depth missing 15,20,24-26 Oct.
11. Upstox Plus effect on REST limits and on ltpc/greeks/full subscription counts: not stated (BO Upstox lines 81, 85, 114).
12. Kite Personal(Free) access to websocket/quote: UNKNOWN (BO Kite line 90).
13. NSE: scripted collection forbidden by its own terms (BO NSE line 131); a live NSE chain recorder would need written permission.
